Our Ecommerce Growth Method

Diagnose the bottleneck, repair the funnel, build retention, and then scale.

Our ecommerce growth method starts with a simple question: is the next dollar more useful in the ad account, or in fixing what happens after the click? Sometimes the right answer is creative testing. Often it is the store experience, the offer or the repeat-purchase system.

Step 1. Find the bottleneck

Review product economics, sessions, the add-to-cart journey, checkout completion, order value, new-versus-returning customers and where paid clicks are landing. Compare channels without treating every platform-reported conversion as incremental revenue.

What we need to see

  • Store revenue and order data broken down by date and product.
  • Traffic sources, landing pages and conversion events.
  • Current acquisition creative and offers.
  • Existing email and post-purchase flows.

Step 2. Fix the funnel before forcing scale

Improve message match from ads to landing pages, strengthen product education, reduce unnecessary decisions and test meaningful offers. For some stores that means stronger product-page modules. For others it means a new funnel, bundle or landing page.

Step 3. Capture demand you have already paid for

Not everyone purchases on the first visit. Email capture should exchange something useful for permission and connect to a relevant welcome sequence. Abandoned checkout and browse follow-ups should be helpful, not endless discount reminders.

Step 4. Build repeat revenue

Post-purchase education, replenishment timing where appropriate, intelligent cross-sells, customer service and a carefully designed loyalty system can create more revenue without buying the same customer again.

Step 5. Grow deliberately

Only after the economics are clear should spend grow. Segment learning by campaign objective and watch new-customer acquisition cost, margin, blended performance and cash flow. A stable business is more valuable than one good screenshot.
