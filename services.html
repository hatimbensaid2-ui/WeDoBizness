How the work is structured

1. Diagnose where the profit leaks

We review acquisition costs alongside conversion rate, average order value, returning customers and the way paid traffic enters the site. That separates a traffic-quality problem from a site or offer problem.

2. Fix the purchase path

Messaging, product information, offer design and the checkout journey are prioritized by potential impact and implementation effort. An effective funnel makes it easier for shoppers to understand why they should buy.

3. Set up retention

Welcome emails, abandoned checkout recovery, post-purchase education and relevant win-back messages can help a store earn more from people it has already reached. We avoid sending the same promotion to everyone.

4. Manage acquisition with useful numbers

Paid media works best when the store measures more than platform ROAS. We look at contribution economics and first-party store data where available. The objective is profitable growth, not impressive-looking dashboards.
