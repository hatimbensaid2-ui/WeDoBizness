Menswear: fixing the offer before scaling

The published example describes an average-order-value increase from $74 to $195 and a ROAS change from 0.83x to 1.56x after reworking offer economics and reducing spend. It illustrates why more advertising is not always the first fix.

How to evaluate any agency case study

Ask for the timeframe, what data was available, whether reported revenue refers to store orders or platform attribution, and how acquisition cost affected contribution margin. The story should explain the decisions and tradeoffs, not just display the highest number on a chart.
