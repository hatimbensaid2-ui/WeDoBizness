# WeDoBizness — Brand System

## Idea
A quiet, capable operator. Minimal and trustworthy, never a hype shop. We build the machine, not the noise.

## Logo
A rising line that reads as both a "W" and a growth / conversion chart. The wordmark keeps **Biz** in green as a subtle accent. SVG mark path:

```

```

Files: `assets/mark.svg`, `assets/favicon.svg`.

## Typography
- **Space Grotesk** (500–700) — logo, headlines, big numbers, labels.
- **Inter** (400–600) — body and UI.
- **Anton** — heavy display headlines, used on ad creatives only.
- **Noto Sans CJK SC** — Simplified Chinese.

## Voice
Operator, not marketer. Direct, plain, numbers over adjectives. No hype, no emojis.
We say: "More profit from the traffic you already pay for." / "We build the structure. You keep the customers."
We don't say: "Explode your sales overnight!" / "Guaranteed 10x, no risk."

## Copy rule
**No em-dashes (—).** Use periods, commas, or colons instead.

## Case-study data (use anonymously)
"One client store": $809K in 8 months, +218% YoY, $1.43M lifetime, 14.9K orders, 1.39M sessions, 0.73% conversion. Never publish raw order lists (real customer names). Only aggregate KPIs / the sales chart are safe to show.
